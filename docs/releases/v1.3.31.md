# v1.3.31 · 2026-09-28

The best-of-best catalog now offers independent ping, YouTube, Telegram and AI
sorting switches, all enabled by default. Successful selected services rank
first, then lower fresh proxy ping when enabled. Individual P/YT/TG/AI indicators
distinguish missing, skipped, inconclusive, stale, successful and failed checks.

“Hide untested” defaults on; “Hide failed” defaults off and applies to confirmed
current failures among selected criteria. Existing members remain accessible
under these filters. Search/sorting preserve the full membership and its routing
order. Preferences persist in browser-local storage. Details:
[`../best-of-best-catalog.md`](../best-of-best-catalog.md).

This v1.3.31 is the catalog update following published v1.3.30. The previously
removed unpublished Jitsi experiment is not part of this build.
